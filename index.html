About Me

My name is Niko Gomez and I am a programmer and game developer. I started making games with Game Maker back in 5th grade and continued through high school to teach myself the essentials (sound, 2D/3D art, animation, programming, design, etc.).


Since 2020 I have been a gameplay engineer at Disruptive Games, where I built the AI encounter system and the physics-based salvaging tooling for Wreck Runners, and also shipped work on Gotham Knights, Diablo II: Resurrected, and Frostpoint VR. From 2025 to 2026 I built Mito, a plant-sim automation roguelike, with a partner on weekends under our label Ink Deer Games; the demo is live on Steam. Before Disruptive I was a software engineer at Penn Interactive Ventures and a Unity/VR engineer at Radd3.

Projects

@ Disruptive Games

Gameplay Engineer · 2020 – Present

Gameplay engineering in Unreal across four shipped titles. Alongside the three below I integrated the Steam Online Subsystem into the UI widgets — friends list, session invites, and the rest — for Frostpoint VR: Proving Grounds.

Wreck Runners
Disruptive Games · Unreal Engine 5 · Released Jul 2026

A 2–4 player physics-based co-op extraction game set in a haunted, ever-changing Bermuda Triangle. I designed and built the AI encounter system — wave spawning, dynamic difficulty, and NPC behaviors — plus the physics constraint system and designer tooling behind the “gravity gun” style salvaging tool. I also modified Unreal's character movement and camera systems to keep the player experience stable aboard the ORCA, the crew's fly / drive / dive utility craft.

View on Steam
Gotham Knights
Disruptive Games · Unreal Engine · 2022

Warner Bros.' co-op action RPG starring Batgirl, Nightwing, Red Hood, and Robin. I built out the animation tooling and the inverse kinematics systems for the player characters.

Diablo II: Resurrected
Disruptive Games · Backend · 2021

Blizzard's remaster of the classic ARPG and its Lord of Destruction expansion. I worked primarily on backend systems — character save data migration and GDPR compliance.

@ Ink Deer Games

Co-founder & Engineer · 2025 – 2026
Mito
Ink Deer Games · Godot 4 / GDScript · Demo on Steam

A plant-sim automation roguelike. You start as a single hand-watered seed and grow into a sprawling network that fuels its own growth — building transport systems, balancing resource flow, and spreading across procedurally generated biomes.


Mito is a two-person project, built on weekends over seven months. I wrote 63K lines of GDScript with Claude Code — a layered simulation of player movement, physical simulation, and resources on a hex grid, wrapped in a roguelike meta-game of escalating hazards and 70+ mutations on an expandable genome grid.

Personal

Side Projects · 2014 – 2021
Tunnel Vision
Personal · VR · 2021

A VR side project in which you see the whole world through the frame you are riding inside. You sit within a buckyball — a geodesic lattice of struts and glowing nodes — and reach out with tracked hands to grab the cage and tumble it through red-rock canyons and open desert.

Stratos
Personal · Unity · 2017

This is Stratos, a team deathmatch shooter in which players pilot ships and jump between planets in orbit. My goal with Stratos was to master Unity's built-in networking solution.

Play the Demo
Last Resort
UC Berkeley GDD Decal · Team Lead

Last Resort is a multiplayer turn-based Scooby Doo. The players are stranded in a mansion where a monster is on the hunt. It was a project for the Game Design and Development Decal (student class) at UC Berkeley. I was the team lead and worked on game design, level design, UI/UX, and sound design.

4X Trading Card Game
Game Jam Entry · UC Berkeley

I put together a game jam for the Game Design and Development Decal. This was my entry, a prototype 4X trading card game. The idea is that you gain natural resources each turn to build structures from a card atlas which then generate tactical resources.

Song Bird
Personal · VR + Leap Motion · 2014

I created Song Bird, a VR + Leap Motion experience, in collaboration with musician Jonathan Quartin. In it you fly across a procedural cityscape chasing songs. It debuted at the 2014 Bay Area Maker Faire.

Project Ecopoesis
SF NeuroGaming Hackathon · 2014

This is a tool for generating flora by specification of growth variables such as size and color. It began as Project Ecopoesis; a game my team entered at the SF NeuroGaming Hackathon 2014. I later expanded on its feature set, allowing specifications through animation curves.

War Games Risk
UC Berkeley · War Games Class

I built this Risk game for the War Games class taught by students at UC Berkeley. In it 4 armies fight for supremacy across a series of competitions (think Ender's Game).
